About burnout

The WHO describes burnout as an occupational phenomenon caused by chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed.

Exhaustion

Drained, tired all the time, sleeping badly.

Cynicism

Detached and negative about the work and people.

Low efficacy

Self-doubt, more mistakes, less getting done.

Get support

If this feels familiar, talk early to your manager, HR partner, or your employee assistance program. Most burnout is caused by the workplace, not by personal weakness. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
